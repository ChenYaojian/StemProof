\begin{abstract}
Estimates of the classical cost of simulating quantum ``supremacy'' circuits rest on
tensor-network contraction: the observed optimal strategies are \emph{stem-shaped}
(state-vector-style sweeps), with cost $\Theta(2^{\min(n,\sqrt{n}\,d)})$ for $n$ qubits and
depth $d$. We present the first machine-checked development of this lower-bound story, in
Lean~4 over Mathlib ($\sim$2{,}100 lines, 16 modules, no \texttt{sorry}, and --- after a
soundness repair we describe --- \textbf{zero custom axioms}: \texttt{\#print axioms} of every
theorem reports only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}).
The development contributes:
(1)~\emph{Contraction Rigidity}, a structural theorem replacing the usual average-case
random-matrix analysis --- any circuit \emph{capable} of entangling across a cut has full
Schmidt rank for all gate parameters outside a proper subvariety --- with the hypothesis
reduced, via Kronecker routing, to a purely combinatorial cross-cut matching;
(2)~a self-contained pathwidth lower bound via an interval-Helly bramble argument,
instantiated on the genuine grid graph;
(3)~a \emph{faithful} circuit model whose contraction orders are real path decompositions of
the spacetime lattice --- widths are derived, not postulated --- pinning the optimum to
$27 \le \cc \le 54$ at the Sycamore-53 scale;
(4)~an end-to-end theorem \lstinline|sycamore53_lower_bound| assembling cut size, the
$10^{18}$ cost estimate, stem optimality, and generic rank $2^{53}$; and
(5)~a cautionary methodological result: the standard ``cite published theorems as axioms''
practice, stated over an abstract structure, made the ambient theory \emph{inconsistent}
(we exhibit machine-checked derivations of \texttt{False}), and we show how hypothesis-form
statements repair it while \emph{strengthening} the headline claims.

%% 2. Practice: optimal paths are "stems" (Huang et al.); cost ~ 4·2^53·steps ≈ 10^18 for
%%    Sycamore-53. But the *lower bound* story (why no contraction does better, and why
%%    rank does not collapse) is scattered across heuristics, probabilistic arguments,
%%    and cited graph theory. No part of it was machine-checked.
%% 3. This paper: a Lean 4 formalization of the full chain -- with an honest accounting of
%%    what is proved, what is hypothesis, and what a first "cite as axiom" attempt got

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Contraction Rigidity (\S\ref{sec:rigidity}).} A structural reframing of the
    average-case tightness question: \lstinline|BulkEntangling| $\Rightarrow$
    \lstinline|ContractionRigid| --- one full-rank gate configuration upgrades to generic
    full rank (Schwartz--Zippel, no probability). The hypothesis is discharged by a purely
    combinatorial cross-cut matching via Kronecker routing; random circuits and Sycamore
    become corollaries.
  \item \textbf{Self-contained pathwidth lower bound (\S\ref{sec:bramble}).} Stem orders are
    path decompositions, so the width lower bound needs only \emph{interval} Helly --- not
    the subtree Helly that treewidth requires and Mathlib lacks. We prove the bramble bound
    abstractly and instantiate it on the genuine nearest-neighbour grid
    ($\mathrm{pathGraph}\,k \mathbin{\square} \mathrm{pathGraph}\,k$).
  \item \textbf{A faithful model (\S\ref{sec:model}).} Contraction orders are \emph{real}
    path decompositions of the spacetime lattice; width is the actual largest bag. Nothing
    is postulated: the lower bound is the bramble theorem, the upper bound an explicit
    sliding-window sweep of width $k+1$ (the textbook grid pathwidth), pinning
    $27 \le \cc \le 54$ at scale $53$.
  \item \textbf{End-to-end Sycamore theorem (\S\ref{sec:endtoend}).} One theorem conjoining
    cut $=53$, cost in $[10^{18},10^{19})$, stem optimality on the faithful model, and
    generic Schmidt rank $2^{53}$ --- standard axioms only.
  \item \textbf{Geometric routing (\S\ref{sec:routing}).} The cross-cut matching at scale
    $\min(n,\sqrt{n}\,d)$ is realized by gate counting (the $b\cdot d$ cap is a theorem,
    not an assumption) and lightcone reachability on 1D chains and 2D chips.
  \item \textbf{Axioms considered harmful (\S\ref{sec:axioms}).} Our first iteration cited
    three published graph theorems as global axioms over an abstract structure; all three
    are falsifiable by adversarial instances, and \texttt{False} was derivable. We document
    the repair --- named \lstinline|Prop|s supplied per model, discharged by computation on
    the concrete model --- and argue it should be the default idiom.
\end{itemize}

\section{The End-to-End Sycamore Theorem}\label{sec:endtoend}
%% CorollaryD.lean (pure arithmetic): sweepCut 53 20 = 53; 10^18 <= 4·2^53·150 < 10^19.
%% Sycamore.lean: the four-conjunct theorem; #print axioms = standard only.
\begin{lstlisting}
theorem sycamore53_lower_bound :
    CorollaryD.sweepCut 53 20 = 53 ∧
    ((10:ℕ)^18 ≤ CorollaryD.stemCost 53 20 150 ∧
      CorollaryD.stemCost 53 20 150 < (10:ℕ)^19) ∧
    ((∃ o, sycamore53.IsStem o ∧
        sycamore53.width o = sycamore53.cc) ∧
      (∀ o, 53 ≤ 2 * sycamore53.width o) ∧
      (∃ o, sycamore53.width o ≤ 54)) ∧
    (∃ e T, ContractionRigid leftBlock e T)
\end{lstlisting}

\section{Axioms Considered Harmful}\label{sec:axioms}
%% The CPP-audience payload. Tell it straight:
%% (a) v1 declared: axiom markovShi (G) : G.cc = G.tw;
%%     axiom latticePathwidthBound (G) (h : G.IsLattice) : G.pw <= C·G.cc;
%%     axiom treewidthLowerBound ... ;
%% (b) tw / IsLattice are *free fields* of the abstract structure -> adversarial
%%     instances falsify all three; we exhibit the three-line False derivations
%%     (machine-checked, reproduced in the appendix).
%% (c) The repair: named Props (MarkovShi G, LatticePathwidthBound G C) supplied per
%%     model; on the concrete model they are *discharged by computation* (C = 1), so the
%%     end-to-end theorem needs no axiom at all -- strictly stronger than v1 claimed.
%% (d) Lesson: "cite, don't reprove" must quantify over faithful models, never over
%%     abstract structures with free semantic fields. Hypothesis-form > axiom-form:
%%     the reader sees the citation in the statement, and #print axioms stays clean.
\begin{lstlisting}
-- v1 (UNSOUND): False was derivable
axiom latticePathwidthBound (G : CircuitGraph)
    (hlat : G.IsLattice) : G.pw ≤ C * G.cc
-- v2 (sound): a named Prop, supplied per model
def LatticePathwidthBound (G : CircuitGraph) (C : ℕ) :
    Prop := G.pw ≤ C * G.cc
\end{lstlisting}
